About this game

Your first batches are entirely hands-on. There's plenty to keep you busy, from getting the recipe just right to keeping on top of the cleaning. After all, a dirty brewery isn't going to win anyone over.

From a single kettle to the town's favorite brewery.

It all starts with an empty warehouse, a single kettle, and a fermenter. Haul in sacks of grain, mash your ingredients, boil the wort, transfer it to the fermenter, and wait for it to do its thing. Once it's ready, it's time to bottle or keg your beer.

Run your own taproom. Set your own prices.

Stock the fridge, serve pints, put together six-packs, and decide what everything costs. At the end of each day, see how much you've sold, what it cost to make, and how your reputation is shaping up.

Become part of the town.

Head out into Oakhaven and get to know the locals. Before long, the corner shop is stocking your beer, the local pub is pouring your pints, and that farmer down the road is supplying your barley.

Build the brewery you've always wanted.

Invest in bigger equipment, add more fermenters, and bring in a craft bottling line. Knock through walls, expand into the upstairs, and arrange your equipment however you like.

You don't have to do it all yourself.

Hire brewers to help with production and taproom staff to keep the customers happy. Give your employees work orders, set priorities, and let them get on with things.

Better together.

The entire game works in online co-op. Split up the responsibilities, with one person brewing, another running the taproom, and someone else handling deliveries.
